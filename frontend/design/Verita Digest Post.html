Anthropic Isolates Skill Induction Heads in 70B Models
transformer-circuits.pub · 4h ago
  • Researchers identified a cluster of ~140 attention heads that fire selectively on coherent in-context examples, providing the clearest mechanistic account of few-shot learning to date.
  • Ablating the cluster degraded few-shot performance to near-zero while leaving single-pass completion largely intact.
  • Result localises a capability that had resisted mechanistic treatment for three years of prior work.
  • The paper includes activation patching diagrams and open-weights reproduction code for all major 70B families.

Goodfire Finds Sparse Features That Predict Refusal Before It Happens
goodfire.ai · 8h ago
  • Using their SAE tooling, Goodfire identified a small set of features in residual stream mid-layers that reliably activate 2–4 tokens before a refusal output begins.
  • First time pre-refusal internal states have been characterised at feature granularity in a production-scale model.
