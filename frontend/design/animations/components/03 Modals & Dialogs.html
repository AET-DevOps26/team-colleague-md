Auth modal — open / tabs / forgot / shake

Modes
Login · Signup · Forgot
Tab switch
Horizontal push, forward = left
Submit
800ms loading → success overlay → auto-close
Dismiss
Esc · backdrop click · close button

Press Esc or click the dim backdrop to dismiss. Shake fires whether or not the modal is open — if closed, it opens to login first.

Settings modal — mid-size

Panel
440px wide · scrolls on overflow
Motion
160ms backdrop fade · 160ms translateY + scale
Internal
No panel push — flat content

Confirm dialog — narrow variant

Panel
320px wide · no header bar
Actions
Cancel + Confirm row at bottom · danger token when destructive
Motion
Inherits settings modal (160ms)

Choosing Delete simply closes — no toast firing here, that's C04's job.

React hint

<Modal> · <ConfirmDialog> · <AuthModal>, with useModalStack() for z-index, focus return, and Esc dispatch. Key props: open, onClose, dismissOn, blockDismiss (mid-submit), disableMotion. <AuthModal> adds mode: 'login' | 'signup' | 'forgot' and onSuccess(kind). Files: src/components/Modal/{Modal,ConfirmDialog}.tsx · src/components/Auth/AuthModal.tsx. Esc, backdrop click, and × button are wired on every modal; the auth modal blocks dismiss while submitting. Reduced motion follows tokens.css's global override.
