Digest card · CTA arrow shift

Markup
article.card.digest-card > .digest-card-link > label · title · bullets · cta
Hover
No tile lift. CTA color → #fff · .cta-arrow translateX(3px)
Surface
black #0A0A0A · aspect-ratio: 5 / 6 · serif italic title at 22px
Why no lift
digest already differentiates by surface (dark, taller, denser). Lifting it competes with the image cards next to it in the masonry.

The digest is structurally a different object — a packaged set of picks, not a single post — so its hover signature is signal in the CTA, not motion on the tile.
