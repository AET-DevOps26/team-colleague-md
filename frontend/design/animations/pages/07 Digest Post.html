Section reveal stagger Extended

Production renders all .topic-event blocks together — no entrance motion. Proposed: opacity + translateY(6px) over 220ms cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1), 80ms stagger, capped at 5 visible items (longer digests skip the cascade past item 5 to avoid a 2-second unfold). Tempo borrows from C09's fadeIn family (180ms / 60ms) but slowed because digest items are dense, headline-bearing blocks rather than comment rows — they need a touch more weight on arrival.

TOC sticky tracking Extended

Production has no table-of-contents primitive. Proposed: a sidebar list anchored position: sticky; top: 0 with active-section tracking via IntersectionObserver watching each .topic-event-headline. Active entry transitions color + background over 160ms ease-out — pure color is safe under reduced-motion. The observer's rootMargin is "0px 0px -70% 0px" so the entry highlights when the headline crosses the upper third of the viewport, not when it just barely enters.

Item card hover Production

Each .topic-event hover lifts the row 1px and brightens the border — same family as C05's image-card lift (translateY(-1px), 160ms ease-out). The trailing .topic-event-link svg shifts translateX(2px) on hover (pages/Verita Digest Post.html:370–371) — the same arrow-shift idiom as C05's digest-card CTA, just at smaller amplitude (2px instead of 3px) because digest items are denser than feed cards.
