Search-input focus Production

34px height, 8px radius, 3px focus shadow at rgba(10,10,10,0.06) — the shortest field in the system, sourced 1:1 from pages/Verita Admin.html:204–212. Co-transitions border-color, background, and box-shadow over 120ms. Cited from C02 §6.

Tab into the field or click it. Width-cap is 240px in production; the bg flip from --bg-surface → #fff is the surface-context cue (admin sits on tinted base, so flipping to white reads as "active").

Tab switch Production

Moderation ↔ Users tab swap is an instant display:none flip on .tab-panel — no panel slide. The animated parts are the .tab-btn (color + border-bottom-color, 120ms) and the inline .tab-count (background --bg-elevated → --accent, color → #fff, 120ms). Sourced 1:1 from pages/Verita Admin.html:168–182; canonicalized in C06 §3.

Role select & action menu Production

Two anchored affordances on a Users-table row. The native <select> (:331–338) reuses the search-input focus signature (3px shadow, bg flip, accent border) — one focus vocabulary across all admin fields. The ⋯ action menu mirrors C07 §3's manage-portal: @keyframes dropIn 120ms over opacity + translateY(-4px) + scale(0.97). Destructive items get the only red hover surface in the system (#FEF2F2).

In production the menu is portaled to document.body to escape table overflow; demoed inline. Destructive items fire C03's confirm dialog (320px .modal--narrow) before the row plays @keyframes rowFade 400ms and collapses.
