Field-box focus Production

The .field-box is the densest motion contract in the auth flow — and the divergent one in the system. 44px height, 8px radius, --bg-elevated background that does not flip on focus (every other field family on a tinted surface does). Only the border-color animates, 120ms linear. Cited 1:1 from pages/Verita Auth.html:128–139; canonicalized in C02 §1.

Step panel transition Production

The auth modal is a single pane that re-mounts its content per step (login → forgot → OTP). Production does not slide sideways — it forces a reflow and re-fires the same modalIn keyframe (200ms cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1), opacity + translateY(8px) + scale(0.98)). Cited from Verita Auth.html:97–100 (keyframe) and :327–330 (the reflow trick).

OTP cell focus shift Production

Each cell is 52px / 8px / --bg-elevated. The .focused modifier (applied JS-side when the underlying input has focus and the cell is empty) flips border to --text-primary, paints a 4px shadow rgba(10,10,10,0.06), and the bg to #fff. A 1.5px caret blinks at 1s steps(2). Cited 1:1 from Verita Auth.html:160–175.

useOTPInput(length=6) returns { cells, value, isComplete }. The hook owns the focus-shift logic; cells are presentational.

Submit + error states Extended

Production wires error reveal as a display: flex flip on .field-error + .error class on .field-box (border → danger). No shake, no spinner, no success morph exist in production — these are extended proposals that line up with C01 §press and C02 §error-shake (320ms 3-cycle, ±4px). The success ✓ stroke-draw (280ms) replaces the route-change confirmation that today is silent.
