Sticky action bar Production

Action bar lives at the bottom of the article body. When the title leaves viewport, it transitions to position: sticky; bottom: 24px with a subtle box-shadow fade (200ms ease). Content underneath gains padding-bottom equal to the bar height to avoid overlap.

In production, useStickyActionBar() watches the title element via IntersectionObserver and toggles the .stuck class — never measures scroll directly.

Back transition Extended

Reverse of forward push: detail slides right (translateX 0 → 100%, 460ms ease-ios), Home slides in from left, scroll position restored from history.state.scrollY. The action bar fades out at t=0 rather than translating with the surface, so it doesn't smear during the slide.

Not yet implemented — Plan calls this Phase B (page transitions). Currently the back button is a hard route swap.
