404 mount cascade Extended

Production paints the 404 card synchronously — there is no entrance motion in pages/Verita 404.html. The Extended proposal is a 280ms ease-out fade + 6px translateY cascade across the five vertical elements (kicker → numeral → headline → body → CTA row) at a 60ms step, total 520ms. Why 280ms (not 180ms): the surface is sparse — five elements alone on a page need a touch more weight on arrival than a comment row.

Transient error toast Extended

For non-fatal failures (network blip, optimistic save reject, background refresh failed), surface as a C04 variant: 'error' toast in the bottom-right stack — not a full error page. useErrorToast() wraps the C04 showToast primitive, defaulting to role="alert" + 4000ms lifetime + hover-pause + manual close. The page itself stays on its current state; the toast is the only surface that changed.

Inline form error Production

Submit-with-invalid sequence: field-box border flips to --danger over the production 120ms border-color transition (C02 §1, sourced from pages/Verita Auth.html:126–139); the Extended shake (C02 §9) plays simultaneously over 320ms; the message reveals 320ms later via opacity + translateY(-2px → 0) over 180ms. Typing into the field clears the error immediately — never re-shake on every keystroke.

Sequencing matters: shake fires first so the user notices motion at the failed input; the message reveal is delayed 320ms so it doesn't slide in under a vibrating box.

Recovery CTA Production

The 404 page's two CTAs are sourced 1:1 from pages/Verita 404.html:120–147. .btn-primary transitions background 120ms ease-out from --accent to --accent-hover; .btn-ghost transitions color 120ms ease-out from --text-secondary to --text-primary. No translate, no scale, no press feedback — production has no :active state on either button (C01 Inconsistencies §4).

When C01 §4 (Extended press scale 0.97) lands as the unified <Button> contract, the 404 CTAs retire their inline transition rules and inherit it. Until then, preserve the 120ms literal for fidelity.
